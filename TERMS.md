## Statistics and availability

Counts measure observed inputs, not successful casts or actions. Captures can be partial, session detection can fail and game updates can break integrations. VALORANT agent selection is manual; locked cursor positions limit its cursor maps. Statistics are not official match records.

Read [Privacy](PRIVACY.md) before enabling capture or sharing a summary. This project does not provide a hosted account service, paid subscription or guarantee of continued compatibility.
